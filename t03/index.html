T3｜代名詞與數量詞表

代名詞是會考文法第二常考（6 年 9 題）：選 I／me／mine、it／one、the other、both／all。

1. 人稱代名詞 第 20 夜

主格（當主詞）受格（動詞、介系詞後）所有格（＋名詞）所有格代名詞（＝所有格＋名詞）反身（自己）
I 我memyminemyself
you 你youyouryoursyourself
he 他himhishishimself
she 她herherhersherself
it 它、牠itits—itself
we 我們usouroursourselves
you 你們youyouryoursyourselves
they 他們themtheirtheirsthemselves
  • This chair isn't mine. It's hers.（mine＝my chair，後面不再接名詞）
  • 主詞和受詞是同一個人才用反身：I told myself to be brave.　｜　its（它的）≠ it's（it is）

2. it／one／ones／them 第 20 夜

it就是前面那一個I found my ticket. I put it in my bag.
one同一類的任何一個My pen is broken. Can I borrow one?
ones同一類的其他幾個These bags are small. Try the bigger ones.
them就是前面那一批Where are my keys? I can't find them.

3. one…the other／another 第 21 夜

one…the other兩個中的一個、另一個One cup is blue; the other is white.
one…the others一個、其餘全部One is here; the others are outside.
another再一個（不限哪一個）We need another cup.
others（不特定的）其他人、其他的Some like tea; others like water.

4. both／all／each／either／neither 第 21、28 夜

both兩個都（複數動詞）Both of them are ready.
all三個以上都All of the students came.
each／every每一個（單數動詞）Each student has a cup.
either兩者之一You can take either bus.
neither兩者都不Neither of us was brave.

5. some／any 與數量詞 第 21 夜、S10

some／something肯定句、請別人吃喝Would you like some tea?
any／anything否定句、問句I don't have anything left.
many／a few／few＋可數複數（few＝幾乎沒有）I have a few friends here.
much／a little／little＋不可數（little＝幾乎沒有）There is a little water.
a lot of／lots of可數、不可數都可以She has a lot of books.
